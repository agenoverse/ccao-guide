This page brings together the resources that Shazil Khan recommends for preparing
for the Panaversity PCAO-F and Anthropic CCAO-F exams. Use the sequence below
to move from the complete picture, through course study and practice, to final
revision and mock exams.

## Step 1: See the complete picture

Start with the [CCAO-F & PCAO-F Master Mind Map](https://ccao.agenoverse.org/docs/master-map/).
It gives you the complete picture before you begin studying individual courses.

## Additional exam resources

### Shazil Khan's study guide

The [CCAO-F & PCAO-F Exam Prep guide](https://ccao.agenoverse.org/) is the main
study record. It brings together study guides, practice questions, course
material, vocabulary, and a recommended preparation path.

### Ageno Quiz

[Ageno Quiz](https://quiz.agenoverse.org/) is the assessment application for
practice and timed quizzes. You can select a quiz or use an access code, save
your attempt, answer against the clock, and receive a score with a
question-by-question review when the quiz allows it.

### Saad's NotebookLM study notebooks

[NotebookLM Study Notebooks](https://ccao.agenoverse.org/docs/study-notebooks/)
provides ready-made notebooks for the seven CCAO-F domains. Use them to learn a
domain deeply, practise exam-style questions, and revisit weak areas with
starter prompts.

You can also open the [Agenoverse YouTube workshop playlist](https://youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZA8X9QQpeWM&si=xW2p3MjfxK6TGfpQ)
for a five-day CCAO-F preparation workshop series.

### Taha Shabbir's videos

Use [Taha Shabbir's YouTube channel](https://www.youtube.com/@tahashabbirai)
as an additional video-learning resource while preparing for the exam.

## Recommended workflow

Use this simple cycle throughout your preparation:

1. **Study** the relevant guide, mind map, or scenario explanation.
2. **Practise** with the course quiz or practice questions.
3. **Diagnose** the concepts and question types you found difficult.
4. **Revisit** the relevant vocabulary, NotebookLM notebook, or scenario guide.
5. **Validate** your readiness with the concept revision practice and final mock
   exam.
