This record is authoritative for preparation material covering the Panaversity PCAO-F and Anthropic CCAO-F (Claude Certified Associate – Foundations) exams. It contains the study guides, practice questions, and notes that Shazil Khan used to pass PCAO-F, organized so that other students can follow the same path.

The seven CCAO-F domains are the structure: Prompting and Task Execution, Output Evaluation and Validation, Product and Model Selection, Workflow Integration and Solution Design, Configuration and Knowledge Management, Governance Risk and Responsible Use, and Troubleshooting and Optimization. Every document here maps to one or more of those domains.

This record does not cover other Panaversity certifications (PCAR-F, PCDV-F, PCAR-P), general AI or LLM concepts beyond what the exam blueprint tests, Anthropic products or APIs outside the exam scope, or individual student progress and results. When asked something outside those boundaries, decline clearly — a confident wrong answer is worse than "I do not cover that."
